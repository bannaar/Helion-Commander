# HELION COMMANDER CONTEXT

HELION Commander is an official companion client for HELION. It is not a standalone authoritative universe.

## Setting snapshot
Severance: 187 AS.
Present/game start: 1087 AS.
Elapsed time: exactly 900 years.

Known Space: exactly 200 persistent systems.
- Helion Commonwealth: 27
- Solar Directorate: 25
- Free Systems Compact: 25
- Meridian League: 23
- Aurelian Synod: 20
- Faction-controlled: 120
- Zero Space: 80

Riftspace and broader Unknown/Deep Unknown are outside the 200-system count.

## Institutions and terms
- Inter-Galactic Governance (IGG): non-sovereign treaty/service framework.
- Coequal IGG arms: INSA, Gatewatch, Galactic Defense Force (GDF).
- Currency: Galactic Standard Credit (GSC).
- Interstellar information infrastructure: UniNet.
- Commonwealth capital: Crownspire.
- Heavy industrial/manufacturer identity: Ironstar Forge.
- Lost agricultural world: Verdance.
- 0.0 Known Space frontier: Zero Space.
- The Null: separate cosmic/existential mystery.
- Formal advanced-tech term: Adaptive Systems Architecture (ASA).

## Player knowledge
Physical truth belongs to the server. A commander receives only authorized read models.
Where implemented, discovery may progress DETECTED -> CLASSIFIED -> LOCATED -> SURVEYED.

Never expose undiscovered anomalies, secret routes, private organization intelligence, admin/moderation data, credentials, database-only fields, or protected mystery answers.

## Companion direction
Public information may include service status, public topology, public security/sovereignty, public definitions, UniNet, and bounded market summaries.

Authenticated information may include only authorized commander profile, owned fleet/loadouts, progression/standings, safe mission summaries, bookmarks/routes, permitted account/market data, discovery knowledge, and organization data allowed by scope.

The current Android prototype is mock-backed. Its seeded names, prices, ships, missions, systems, and organizations are demonstrations unless separately confirmed by current canon or server implementation.
